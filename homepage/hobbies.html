My Hobbies

Things that bring me joy outside of code.

Running clears my head and keeps me grounded. Whether it's an easy morning jog or pushing through a longer distance, there's nothing quite like finishing a run and feeling like you earned the rest of your day.

Current goal:An one hundred meter sprint under 12 seconds.

Cooking is just programming with ingredients: follow the algorithm, adjust parameters, and debug when something smells wrong. I like experimenting with different cuisines and rarely make the same dish twice.

Current obsession: Homemade ramen broth.

Beyond schoolwork, I love building things just to see if I can. Small scripts, mini-games, tools that solve everyday annoyances — coding as a hobby feels completely different from coding as homework.

Latest side project: None at the moment

Fishing is the perfect excuse to sit quietly by the water and do absolutely nothing productive. It's a great reset after a long week of staring at screens.

Favourite spot: Local lake on early weekend mornings.

I've been gaming since I was a kid and it's stuck. I enjoy strategy games, open-world RPGs, and the occasional competitive shooter. Gaming is also what first got me curious about how software actually works under the hood.

Currently playing: Whatever I can squeeze in between homework.
